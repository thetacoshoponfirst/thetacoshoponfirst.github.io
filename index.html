Our story

Born on First St.

The Taco Shop opened on March 30, 2026, just two doors down from Letty's Authentic Mexican Cocina. It's the newest kitchen from Letty Martinez, the owner behind Letty's and Yummy Tummy Pastries. The idea was simple: real Mexican breakfast, starting with tortillas made by hand every morning.
