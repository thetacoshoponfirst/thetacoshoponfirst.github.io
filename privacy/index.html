Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The Taco Shop on First St ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit this website.

Information we collect

This website does not have accounts, sign-up forms or online payments, and we do not ask you for personal information here. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically record basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visited, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use this data to identify you.

Phone orders

When you call us to place an order, we only use the name and details you give us to prepare your order and contact you about it. We do not sell or share this information.

Links to other sites

Our website links to other services, such as DoorDash, Google Maps, Google Reviews, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Linktree. When you use them, their own privacy policies apply, not this one.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
